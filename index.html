Our Heritage

The Art of
Handcrafted Tea

Nestled in the misty highlands of Sri Lanka, LUWIS TEA represents generations of tea-making excellence. Our journey began with a simple promise: to share the authentic taste of Ceylon tea with the world.

Each leaf is hand-picked by skilled artisans who understand the perfect moment of harvest. We follow traditional methods passed down through generations, ensuring every cup delivers the rich flavor and aroma that makes Ceylon tea legendary.

Our commitment to quality, hygiene, and health is unwavering. From the pristine gardens to your cup, we maintain the highest standards of purity and freshness.
